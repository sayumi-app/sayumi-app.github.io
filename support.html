Sayumi Support

Sayumi translates a conversation between two people, out loud. You speak, pause, and Sayumi says it in the other language; then the other person replies.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings (the gear) and choose your partner language.
  2. Tap Download English & … and accept Apple's download prompt if one appears.
  3. Tap the microphone and start talking. Pause to hear the translation.

Common questions

A language says it isn't supported. Apple's built-in translation, speech recognition and voices don't cover every language on every iPhone or iOS version. The note under the language in Settings shows what's missing. If only speech recognition is missing, use Type instead.

It picked the wrong language. Short phrases are harder to identify. Speak a full sentence; if Sayumi isn't sure, it will ask which language was spoken.

I can't hear the translation. Check the volume and that silent mode isn't on. In Settings you can choose a different voice, or download more voices in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Is my conversation saved? No. It stays in memory while the app is open and is erased when you close it. See the Privacy Policy.
