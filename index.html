Sayumi

Say You, Say Me — talk and translate, out loud.

How it works

Two people, two languages, one iPhone

Tap the microphone and talk. Pause, and Sayumi speaks your words in the other language. Then the other person replies, and Sayumi translates them back to you.

English with any of 18 languages, from Arabic and Mandarin to Spanish, Japanese and Ukrainian, all translated on your iPhone with Apple's built-in translation.

Private by design

Translation runs on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and your conversation isn't saved. Read the privacy policy.
