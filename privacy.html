Sayumi Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 November 2026

Sayumi ("the app") is a conversation translator for iPhone, made by Chris Zidanski ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short: Sayumi does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

What the app uses, and why

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, Sayumi listens so it can turn your speech into text for translation. Speech is recognised by Apple's speech recognition built into iOS, on your iPhone where your device supports it.

To work out which language was spoken, the app briefly saves a short recording on your iPhone. It is deleted as soon as recognition finishes, or when you tap Stop. It is never sent to us.

Optional Apple network speech

If on-device recognition isn't available for a language, you can choose to turn on Allow Apple network speech in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your speech may be sent to Apple to be recognised, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive this audio.

Translation and spoken output

Translation uses Apple's Translation framework, which translates on your iPhone. The first time you use a language, iOS may download its translation files from Apple. The translated text is read aloud by voices built into iOS, also on your iPhone.

Your conversation

The conversation shown in the app (up to the last 100 lines) is kept only in your iPhone's memory while the app is open. It is not saved to storage and it is not uploaded. It is erased when you close the app or tap Clear conversation.

Your settings

Your choices, such as your partner language, voices and speaking speed, are saved on your iPhone so the app remembers them. They stay on your device, and they are deleted if you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share data.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your audio, text or translations.

Permissions

Sayumi asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition access so it can hear and understand you. You can change these at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under Sayumi. If you turn them off, you can still use Type instead to translate typed text.

Children

Sayumi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a future option to save conversations, we will update this policy and its effective date before that change ships.
