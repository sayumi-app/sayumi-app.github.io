<h1 align="center">Sayumi</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Say You, Say Me.</b> A free conversation translator for iPhone.</p>

---

Sayumi lets two people talk across a language barrier. You speak, and Sayumi says it out loud in
the other person's language. They answer, and it says their reply back in yours. It's made for
travellers: ordering a coffee, asking directions, checking in.

- **18 languages with English:** Arabic, Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian,
  Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and
  Vietnamese.
- **Works offline** once your languages are downloaded, using Apple's built-in translation.
- **Private:** no account, no ads and no tracking. Your conversation stays on your iPhone.
- **Free,** for iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
